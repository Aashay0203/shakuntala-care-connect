
# Shakuntala Hospital – Healthcare Website

## Overview
A warm, professional healthcare website for Shakuntala Hospital with orange/white/neutral color theme, smooth animations, and patient-friendly design.

## Pages & Sections

### Single-Page Layout with Sticky Navigation
- **Navbar**: Sticky top nav with logo, section links (About, Doctors, Services, Contact), and a persistent "Book Appointment" CTA button. Mobile hamburger menu.

### 1. Hero Section
- Large banner with warm orange gradient background
- Clinic name, tagline "Trusted Care for Every Stage of Life", short description
- Two CTA buttons: "Book Appointment" (scrolls to contact) and "Chat on WhatsApp" (opens wa.me link)

### 2. About Us
- Brief intro focusing on trust, experience, and care for women, men, and children
- Clean layout with icon accents

### 3. Doctors Section
- 4 doctor profile cards (Dr. Manmeet Gupta, Dr. Amita Gupta, Dr. Anand Prakash Gupta, Dr. Vinay Sharma)
- Each card: avatar placeholder, name, qualification, experience, specialization
- Rounded cards with soft shadows

### 4. Services Section
- Icon-based grid (11 services: Maternity Care, General Consultation, Indoor OPD, ECG, X-Ray, Medicines, Lab Tests, Gynecology, Infertility, Child Healthcare, Orthopedic Care)
- Lucide icons for each service

### 5. Experience & Achievements
- Animated counters: Years of Experience, 20,000+ Patients Treated, 20,000+ Successful Treatments
- Number count-up animation on scroll

### 6. Gallery Section
- Grid of placeholder clinic images
- Lightbox preview on click

### 7. Testimonials
- 3-5 dummy patient review cards with name and feedback
- Clean card layout

### 8. Clinic Timing
- Highlighted card showing Mon-Sat 8:00 AM – 8:30 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM

### 9. Location & Contact
- Embedded Google Map iframe for the Bilsanda address
- Contact info with clickable Call, Email, and WhatsApp buttons

### 10. Footer
- Quick links, contact info, copyright

## Design System
- **Primary color**: Warm orange (HSL ~25 95% 55%)
- **Accent**: Soft cream/neutral tones
- **Background**: White and light warm gray
- **Cards**: Rounded corners, soft shadows
- **Typography**: Clean, readable fonts
- Smooth scroll behavior, subtle fade-in animations on scroll
